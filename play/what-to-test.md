# What to test

Thank you for testing Strangely Attractive. Please spend a few minutes with these areas:

1. Open the controls menu and switch between Lorenz, Rössler, Chua, Aizawa, Thomas, and Halvorsen.
2. Change color schemes, visual styles, particle count, speed, and trail settings. Watch for freezes or a blank canvas.
3. Toggle the real 3D view. Drag to rotate and pinch to zoom. The picture should fill the screen.
4. Open the equations and guided lessons panels. The picture should stay visible beside or above a lesson. Check that text remains readable and that Back closes each panel.
5. Turn on the Lyapunov display and let it settle.
6. Tap Export as PNG, save the image, and open it in a gallery or file app.
7. Rotate the device and confirm the visualization fills the new layout and every toolbar button can be reached.
8. Put the device in airplane mode, restart the app, and confirm all core features still work.
9. Try TalkBack, a keyboard, or a large font size. Confirm focus remains visible and controls have useful names.

When reporting a problem, include the device model, Android version, the selected attractor, and what you tapped just before the issue appeared.
