# What to test

Thank you for testing Strangely Attractive. Please spend a few minutes with these areas:

1. Open the controls menu and switch between Lorenz, Rössler, Chua, Aizawa, Thomas, and Halvorsen.
2. Change color schemes, visual styles, particle count, speed, and trail settings. Watch for freezes or a blank canvas.
3. Toggle the real 3D view. Drag to rotate and pinch to zoom.
4. Open the equations and guided lessons panels. Check that text remains readable and every panel closes cleanly.
5. Turn on the Lyapunov display and let it settle.
6. Rotate the device and confirm the visualization fills the new layout.
7. Put the device in airplane mode, restart the app, and confirm all core features still work.
8. Try a keyboard or accessibility service. Confirm focus remains visible and controls have useful names.

When reporting a problem, include the device model, Android version, the selected attractor, and what you tapped just before the issue appeared.
