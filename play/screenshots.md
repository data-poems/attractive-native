# Screenshot inventory and alt text

Real captures of Android 1.0.7 (8), installed from the APKs that `bundletool` cuts from the release bundle, on
Android 16 emulators, November 12, 2026. No device frames, no added text, no mock data. The only processing was
rewriting each PNG from 32-bit to 24-bit (the alpha channel was fully opaque). The readout in each shot shows an
emulator frame rate, not what a device does.

## Phone (1080 x 2160; the landscape shot is 2160 x 1080)

| File | Alt text |
|---|---|
| `01-lorenz.png` | The Lorenz attractor drawn as cyan and magenta trails on a black screen, with the playback toolbar along the bottom and a small readout box. |
| `02-3d-view.png` | The 3D view: the Lorenz attractor as fine cyan and magenta lines, seen at an angle so both wings show. |
| `03-lesson.png` | A guided lesson on sensitive dependence. The 3D picture is at the top; below it the step "They part ways" explains how two almost identical starts split, with Back and Next buttons. |
| `04-equations.png` | The Lorenz equations panel: three differential equations, the meaning of sigma, rho and beta, the Lyapunov exponent and dimension, and notes on the system's origin and dynamics. |
| `05-controls.png` | The controls menu: a preset list, a color scheme picker set to Bioluminescent, a drawing style picker set to Clean, and sliders for particle count and animation speed. |
| `06-blueprint-style.png` | The Lorenz attractor in the Blueprint style: pale lines over a dark blue grid. |
| `07-lyapunov-landscape.png` | Landscape 3D view of the Lorenz attractor with the Lyapunov readout on, showing a largest exponent near 0.90 and the status "settled". The whole toolbar is visible. |
| `08-lessons-list.png` | The Guided Lessons list as a sheet under the 3D picture, showing five of the six lesson titles. |
