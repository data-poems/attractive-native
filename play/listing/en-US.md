## App name

Strangely Attractive

## Short description

Explore strange attractors, equations, lessons, and chaos in 2D and 3D.

## Full description

Strangely Attractive turns the equations of chaos into something you can watch, rotate, and reshape.

Follow the Lorenz butterfly, Rössler spiral band, Chua double scroll, Aizawa torus knot, and 21 more strange attractors as they draw themselves in real time. Tiny changes can send the path somewhere completely different, yet each system keeps returning to its own unmistakable shape.

Explore the math from several angles:

- Switch between a clean canvas and a real 3D phase-space view
- Read the differential equations behind each attractor
- Tune parameters, trail length, glow, speed, and color
- Compare visual styles from blueprint and chalk to neon and watercolor
- Follow guided lessons about sensitive dependence and Lyapunov exponents
- Pair attractors with bundled example datasets to see how parameter mappings change the motion

Everything runs on your device. The Android app needs no account or permissions and does not contact a developer server: its WebView blocks network loads. Optional website or support links open only if you tap them. The app contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.
