## App name

Strangely Attractive

## Short description

Watch 25 strange attractors in 2D or 3D, then change the math behind them.

## Full description

Strangely Attractive draws strange attractors while you watch. Each one is a small set of equations that never settles down and never repeats, yet always traces the same recognizable shape.

Pick one of 25 systems and let it run. The list starts with the Lorenz butterfly, the Rössler band and the Chua double scroll. It goes on through Chen, Lü, Aizawa, Thomas, Halvorsen, Dadras, Sprott and more, and ends with three iterated maps: Clifford, De Jong and Pickover.

What you can do

- Watch on a flat canvas, or switch to a 3D view and turn the attractor around with one finger.
- Change the parameters of the equations with sliders and see the path respond.
- Load one of 21 named presets, such as Lorenz Classic, Rössler Funnel or Chua Double Scroll.
- Choose from 32 color schemes and 7 drawing styles: Clean, Neon, Da Vinci, Chalk, Watercolor, Blueprint and Oscilloscope.
- Set the number of trajectories, the animation speed, trail length, glow and line width.
- Tap the dice for a random combination. Undo steps back through your changes.
- Pause, slow down or speed up the animation at any time.
- Save the picture on screen as a PNG, to a place you choose.

Learn the math

Six short guided lessons cover dynamical systems, fixed points, limit cycles, the route to chaos, sensitive dependence and how chaos is measured. Each step sets up the picture it talks about. In one, two paths start a hundred-thousandth apart and you watch them split.

For Lorenz, Rössler, Aizawa, Thomas, Halvorsen and Chen, an equations panel shows the differential equations, what each parameter means, where the system came from and how it behaves.

Turn on the Lyapunov readout to see a running estimate of the largest Lyapunov exponent, a number that says how fast two nearby paths drift apart.

Private and offline

Everything is inside the app and runs on your device. The app asks for no Android permissions and blocks network access for its own pages. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The only web links are two on the About screen, and they open in your browser only if you tap them.

Good to know

The app works in portrait and landscape on phones and tablets. The data themes in the controls only rename the parameters and load starting values. The app does not use measured or live data.
