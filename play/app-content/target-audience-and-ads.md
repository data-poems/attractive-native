# Target audience, ads, and monetization

## Target audience

Recommended Play selections: **13-15, 16-17, and 18 and over**.

Strangely Attractive is an educational visualization for a general audience, not a product specifically designed for children under 13. Keep it out of the Families program unless the product and policy work for that program is completed.

## Ads and monetization

- Ads: No
- Price: Free
- In-app products: None
- Subscriptions: None
- Play Billing: Not used
