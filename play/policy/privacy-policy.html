Strangely Attractive Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026

Strangely Attractive is a mathematical visualization app by Luke Steuber.

Data collection

Strangely Attractive requires no account and collects no personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive your activity, settings, or device information.

On-device data

The app stores visualization preferences, onboarding state, and lesson progress on your device. Remove this local data through the operating system's app-storage controls or by deleting the app.

Network access

The Android app packages its interface, lessons, equations, fonts, and rendering libraries inside the installation. It requests no Android permissions and blocks network loads from its WebView.

Children

Strangely Attractive collects no personal information from children or adults. The app is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes

Luke Steuber will post policy updates here with a revised effective date.
