# Strangely Attractive Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026

Strangely Attractive is a mathematical visualization app by Luke Steuber.

## Data collection

Strangely Attractive does not require an account and does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive your activity, settings, or device information.

## On-device data

The app stores visualization preferences, onboarding state, and lesson progress on your device. This local data lets the app remember your choices. You can remove it through the operating system's app-storage controls or by deleting the app.

## Network access

The Android app packages its interface, lessons, equations, fonts, and rendering libraries inside the installation. It requests no Android permissions and blocks network loads from its WebView.

## Children

Strangely Attractive does not collect personal information from children or adults. The app is not specifically directed to children under 13.
